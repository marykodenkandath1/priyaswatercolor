About the artist

Hi, I'm Priya

Watercolors of drinks, food, buildings and big days, plus original commissions of the homes and places people love.

My story

Hi! My name is Priya, and I am so excited that you're here! I'm from Colorado and moved to Austin post-grad, where I work in medical devices full time. I've always done art growing up, but recently got back into it, and I'm so glad I did. My pieces are now in local stores around Austin and would love to be a part of your home too :)

Process and materials

Every painting is made by hand in my home, and prints are done in a local Austin shop! Everything ships flat with rigid backing.

  • Paper: Textured, ivory, cold-pressed watercolor sheets
  • Paint: Artist-grade, lightfast pigments
