About the artist

Hi, I'm Priya

Watercolors of drinks, food, buildings and big days, plus original commissions of the homes, pets and places people love.

My story

Hi! My name is Priya, and I am so excited that you're here! I'm from Colorado and moved to Austin post-grad, where I work in medical devices full time. I've always done art growing up, but recently got back into it, and I'm so glad I did. My pieces are now in local stores around Austin and would love to be a part of your home too :)

Process and materials

  • Paper: 100% cotton, 300 gsm paper from Arches and Saunders Waterford. It holds water beautifully and lasts for generations.
  • Paint: artist-grade, lightfast pigments, so colors won't fade when hung away from direct sun.
  • Method: mostly wet-into-wet washes, built up in thin transparent layers, with the white of the paper left for the highlights.

The studio

Every painting is made by hand in my home studio. Prints are limited to 6 of each piece, and everything ships flat with rigid backing.
