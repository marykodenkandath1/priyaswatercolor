About the artist

Hi, I'm Priya

Watercolors of drinks, food, buildings and big days, plus portraits of the houses people love.

My story

[Placeholder bio. Priya to replace.] I started painting with a children's watercolor set and never really stopped. What keeps me coming back is how unpredictable the medium is: you guide the water, but you never fully control it. The happy accidents are often my favourite part of a painting.

Right now I'm selling small runs of prints of my favorite pieces, and painting house portraits to order.

Process and materials

  • Paper: 100% cotton, 300 gsm paper from Arches and Saunders Waterford. It holds water beautifully and lasts for generations.
  • Paint: artist-grade, lightfast pigments, so colors won't fade when hung away from direct sun.
  • Method: mostly wet-into-wet washes, built up in thin transparent layers, with the white of the paper left for the highlights.

The studio

Every painting is made by hand in my home studio. Prints are limited to 6 of each piece, and everything ships flat with rigid backing.
