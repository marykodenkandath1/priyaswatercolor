FAQ and policies

Can't find what you need? Email Priya.

Payment

How do I pay?

You can pay by Venmo (@Pkodenkandath) or Zelle (508-333-4830). For prints, place your order on the checkout page and you'll get an order number. Then send the total by Venmo or Zelle and include your order number in the payment note. Your order ships, or is dropped off, once payment is received.

For commissions, you pay once Priya sends you a photo of your finished painting.

Can't use Venmo or Zelle? Email priyaswatercolors@gmail.com with your order number and Priya will sort out another way to pay.

Shipping

Where do you ship?

Within the United States only. If you're in Austin or Colorado, you can choose free local drop-off at checkout instead, and Priya will email you to arrange it.

How much does shipping cost, and how long does it take?

Shipping is a flat $2. Prints ship within 7 business days of your payment, and you'll get a tracking link by email as soon as your order is on its way.

How are prints packed?

Flat, in a protective sleeve with rigid backing on both sides, inside a sturdy mailer.

Returns

Can I return a print?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery if it's in its original condition. Email us to start a return, and you'll be refunded once it arrives back safely. Return shipping is paid by the buyer.

What if it arrives damaged?

Email a photo of the damage and the packaging within 3 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

Can I return a commission?

Commissions can't be returned because they're originals painted just for you. Priya checks in with you before it's finished, and you only pay once you've seen it.

Caring for your print or painting

How should I frame and hang it?
  • Any type of frame works just fine!
  • Hang it away from direct sunlight, and away from damp rooms like bathrooms.
  • UV-protective glass helps keep the colors bright for longer.
Will the colors look exactly like the photos?

Every screen shows color a little differently. Let me know if you want a picture before purchasing!

What sizes do prints come in?

Every print comes in two sizes: 5 × 7 in for $10 or 8 × 10 in for $20. Landscape pieces are the same sizes turned sideways.

Commissions are done on a 5 × 7 but can be digitized and enlarged per your request. You can keep both!

Are the prints signed?

Each print is initialed (PK). If you would like a date on your commission piece, let me know on the form!

Commission terms

The short version
  • All commissions are 5 × 7 originals, $20–$50 depending on detail.
  • Sending a request is free and doesn't commit you to anything.
  • Priya replies within 3 business days to confirm the price, ask questions, or let you know if it isn't the right fit.
  • There's no deposit. Once your painting is finished, Priya will send you a photo, and you'll pay the full amount by Venmo (@Pkodenkandath) or Zelle (508-333-4830). It ships after payment.
  • Paintings are usually ready about 2 weeks after you send the form. If you need it by a certain date, please let me know on the form!
  • I'll send you a photo when it's done, and if you want any tweaks, I'll do my best!
  • Priya keeps the copyright to the painting. You own the original artwork.
  • If you agreed to it on the form, Priya may share photos of the finished piece. If you didn't, she won't.

Privacy policy

What do you collect, and why?

Only what we need to fulfil your order or commission: your name, email, shipping address and any details, address or photos you send with a commission request. Payments happen on Venmo or Zelle, so this site never sees your payment details.

What happens to my reference photos?

They're emailed to Priya and kept in her private Google Drive, seen only by her, and deleted after your commission is complete or closed.

Analytics and cookies

We use privacy-friendly analytics that don't track you across other websites. We don't sell or share your information.
