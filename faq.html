Shipping

Where do you ship?

Within the United States only.

Returns

Can I return a print?

Yes, within 14 days of delivery if it's in its original condition. Email us to start a return, and you'll be refunded once it arrives back safely. Return shipping is paid by the buyer.

What if it arrives damaged?

Email a photo of the damage and the packaging within 3 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

Can I return a house portrait?

House portraits can't be returned because they're painted just for you. Priya checks in with you before it's finished, and you only pay once you've seen it.

Caring for your print or painting

How should I frame and hang it?
  • Frame it behind glass or acrylic with an acid-free mat, so the glass doesn't touch the paper.
  • Hang it away from direct sunlight, and away from damp rooms like bathrooms.
  • UV-protective glass helps keep the colors bright for longer.
Will the colors look exactly like the photos?

Prints are photographed in daylight, but every screen shows color a little differently. If you'd like an extra photo before buying, just ask.

Commission terms

The short version
  • Sending a request is free and doesn't commit you to anything.
  • Priya replies within 3 business days to confirm a quote, ask questions, or let you know if it isn't the right fit.
  • There's no deposit. Once your painting is finished, you'll get a secure payment link for the full amount, and it ships after payment.
  • Paintings are usually ready about 2 weeks after you send the form. If you need it by a certain date, say so on the form.
  • Small changes before it's finished are included. Major changes after that may cost extra.
  • Priya keeps the copyright to the painting. You own the original artwork.
  • If you agreed to it on the form, Priya may share photos of the finished piece. If you didn't, she won't.

Privacy policy

What do you collect, and why?

Only what we need to fulfil your order or commission: your name, email, shipping address and any details, address or photos you send with a house portrait request. We never see or store your card details. Payments are handled by our secure payment provider.

What happens to my reference photos?

They're emailed straight to Priya, seen only by her, and deleted 12 months after your commission is complete or closed.
