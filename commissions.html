House portraits

A hand-painted watercolor of your home, painted from your photos. A lovely gift for a first home, a family house or a place you're saying goodbye to.

How it works

  1. Send the form

    Share a few photos of the house, the size you'd like and your budget.

  2. Priya confirms

    She replies with a quote and any questions, usually within 3 business days.

  3. She paints it

    Your painting is ready about after you send the form.

  4. You pay

    No deposit. You get a secure payment link once your painting is finished.

  5. It ships

    Packed flat with rigid backing and shipped within the US.
