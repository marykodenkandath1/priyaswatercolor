Commissions

An original watercolor painted from your photos: your home, a pet, a wedding venue or a place you love. Every commission is a 5 × 7 in original, painted by hand just for you.

How it works

  1. Send the form

    Tell Priya what you'd like painted and share a few photos.

  2. Priya confirms

    She replies with the price and any questions, usually within 3 business days.

  3. She paints it

    Your painting is ready about after you send the form.

  4. You pay

    No deposit. You get a secure payment link once your painting is finished.

  5. It ships

    Packed flat with rigid backing and shipped within the US.

Pricing

All commissions are originals. No other sizes.

CommissionPrice

House commissions are a flat price. For everything else, the price depends on the level of detail, and Priya confirms it when she replies to your request.
